About the game

SnakeCo takes the snake game everyone played on their first phone and sends it on a trip through Colombia. Each of its seven places has its own board, obstacles and food: coffee bushes and a hot tinto in the Eje Cafetero, palm trees and a hammock on the Caribbean coast, cannons on Cartagena's walls, conga drums in Cali, flower pots in Medellín, wax palms in the Valle de Cocora and the carnival in Pasto. A short story video opens every place, surprises fill a Memories book, and a cheeky wild snake called La Culebrita tries to steal your arepas.

It is a premium game in the old sense: pay once and get everything. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no internet needed. From 1 December to 5 January the game dresses up for a Colombian Christmas by itself, with lights over every place and Medellín's famous alumbrados.

Features

  • Seven places across Colombia, 21 stars to collect, a champion celebration
  • Regional foods worth different points: arepas, empanadas, pandebono, buñuelos, champús…
  • Story videos, a Memories book, a surprise in every place, Colombian voice reactions (optional)
  • Three paces, Records, six secret challenges, light and dark theme
  • Christmas in Colombia, 1 Dec – 5 Jan: Santa hat for the snake, Christmas lights, Medellín's alumbrados

AI disclosure: the pictures and story videos were made with the help of AI; voices and video sounds were generated with AI audio (Runway); the music and game sounds are made by the game itself.

Ficha en español

SnakeCo toma el juego de la culebrita que todos jugamos en nuestro primer celular y lo pone a recorrer Colombia. Cada uno de sus siete lugares tiene su propio tablero, sus obstáculos y su comida: cafetos y un tinto bien caliente en el Eje Cafetero, palmeras y una hamaca en la Costa Caribe, cañones en las murallas de Cartagena, congas en Cali, materas en Medellín, palmas de cera en el Valle de Cocora y el carnaval en Pasto. Un video corto abre cada lugar, las sorpresas llenan un libro de Recuerdos y una culebrita salvaje, La Culebrita, intenta robarte las arepas.

Es un juego de pago único, como los de antes: pagas una vez y tienes todo. Sin anuncios, sin compras dentro del juego, sin cuenta y sin internet. Del 1 de diciembre al 5 de enero el juego se viste de Navidad colombiana por sí solo, con luces en cada lugar y los alumbrados de Medellín.
