Snake, the way you remember it, on a trip through Colombia

The rules are the ones from your first phone: eat, grow, don't hit the walls or yourself. Everything around them is new.

Seven places across Colombia

Each place has its own board, obstacles and food: coffee bushes in the Eje Cafetero, palm trees on the Costa Caribe, cannons in Cartagena, conga drums in Cali, flower pots in Medellín, wax palms in the Valle de Cocora and lajas in Pasto. Earn three stars in each, 21 in all, and become champion.

  • Eje Cafetero
  • Costa Caribe

  • Medellín

  • Pasto

Real Colombian food

Arepas, empanadas, pandebono, buñuelos, champús, a hot tinto and more, each worth different points. Every place has a surprise too: a tintico that speeds you up, a hammock that slows everything down, a salsa party, a rain of flowers. And watch out for La Culebrita, a wild little snake that wants your arepas.

A story video for every place

The mascot flies from Tallinn to Colombia, and a short video with its own sound opens each place the first time you play there. Skip it whenever you like and watch it again in your Memories book.

Questions

Is SnakeCo free?

No. SnakeCo costs €1.99 once (COP 4,900 in Colombia; Google Play shows the price in your currency). There are no ads and no in-app purchases: you pay once and get every place, video and challenge.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once installed, SnakeCo works without internet. Google Play may check your purchase now and then when you are online.

Does SnakeCo collect my data?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no ads. Your scores and settings stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones and tablets through Google Play. An iPhone version is in development, with no date yet.

Which languages?

English, Spanish and Estonian. The voice reactions are in Colombian Spanish, and you can switch them off.

Is it suitable for kids?

The content is family-friendly. Google Play rates it 13+.

Who makes SnakeCo?

DGM Technology OÜ, a one-person indie studio in Tallinn, Estonia, with a big love for Colombia. Write to snakeco@flashylearning.app.
